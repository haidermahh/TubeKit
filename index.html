1. Information We Do NOT Collect

TubeKit is built on privacy-by-design principles:

  • We do not require users to register or create an account with personal credentials to use core features.
  • We do not access or store private YouTube channel logins, passwords, or personal YouTube account credentials.
  • We do not record, sell, rent, or monetize your personal search history or extracted asset URLs.
  • We do not collect sensitive data such as biometric identifiers, phone numbers, contact books, or precise GPS location.

3. Third-Party Services & SDK Integrations

Our application integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may collect data in accordance with their respective privacy policies to deliver core functionality:

A. YouTube API Services

TubeKit utilizes official YouTube Data API v3 and public oEmbed endpoints to inspect public video metadata. By using TubeKit, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. TubeKit only accesses publicly available data on YouTube.

B. Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to serve optional rewarded video advertisements, enabling creators to earn in-app utility credits. AdMob may process your device's Advertising Identifier (AAID) and basic device information to display relevant ads in compliance with Google Play Policies. For more information, please review the Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

C. OneSignal (Push Notifications)

We use OneSignal to send helpful optional notifications regarding creator trends and daily streak reminders. You may enable or disable notifications at any time via Android System Settings. View the OneSignal Privacy Policy.

4. Android Device Permissions

TubeKit only requests permissions that are strictly necessary to deliver its functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Essential for querying public YouTube metadata, loading thumbnails, and retrieving app updates.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Allows the app to notify you of daily streaks and algorithm updates (User opt-in).
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 & below only): Used solely when you choose to save a thumbnail image or transcript file directly to your device's Downloads folder. (Android 10+ uses secure Scoped Storage without requiring broad storage permissions).
  • VIBRATE: Provides subtle haptic feedback on button interactions for enhanced user experience.

5. YouTube API Services & No-Stream-Ripping Policy

TubeKit strictly respects Google Play Developer Policies and the YouTube Terms of Service:

  • TubeKit does not download, convert, extract, or rip YouTube audio (MP3) or video streams (MP4).
  • TubeKit does not facilitate background playback of YouTube video or audio content.
  • All extracted metadata (transcripts, captions, thumbnails, tags) are public assets accessed strictly in accordance with YouTube API guidelines.

6. Data Retention and User Deletion Rights

Because all your search history, bookmarks, and preferences are stored locally on your device:

  • You can delete all history and favorites at any time directly in the app via Settings > Clear All Cache & History.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all locally stored data from your device.
  • If you have questions regarding data deletion, you may contact our support team at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

TubeKit does not knowingly collect or solicit any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data to our app, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove such information.
